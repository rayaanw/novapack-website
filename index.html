Who We Are

About

NovaPack (Pvt.) Ltd. is a flagship concern of Novapack Group and a trusted manufacturer of industrial polyethylene packaging solutions. We serve large-scale manufacturers with reliable, customised and performance-led packaging products, supported by strong technical expertise and deep market relationships.

With roots in industrial raw-material trading and manufacturing excellence, NovaPack has grown into a scalable platform combining PE film extrusion, bag production, sustainable recycling, renewable energy integration and customer-focused service.

  • 50 years of operating experience
  • Qualified and experienced team
  • Focus on optimization
  • Strong values & ethics
  • Customer-centric approach
  • Customised, made-to-order production

Sustainability & Circular Economy

Responsible Manufacturing, Built on Large-Scale Recycling

NovaPack has developed recycling operations in Punjab and Sindh, transforming polyethylene waste into value-added industrial materials. This capacity reinforces our role as a sustainability-focused manufacturer for customers that increasingly require credible environmental practices.

20,000 MT Annual PE recycling capacity — supporting circular manufacturing and responsible industrial growth
  • GRS and RCS certifications supporting recycled-content assurance
  • EPA-approved recycling operations in key regions
  • Halal Food Packaging Certification for compliant food-grade solutions
  • 300 kW solar installed — a further 300 kW (Phase II) in the pipeline
  • Recycled-content and biodegradable product options where applicable

For large industrial customers, packaging is part of quality assurance, supply continuity and ESG accountability. NovaPack’s manufacturing and recycling platform helps customers protect product integrity while strengthening their own sustainability profile.
